Built in Kotlin · Material 3

Every app is a native Android build (Kotlin, minSdk 26, targetSdk 35), signed with a release keystore and shipped through Google Play Console.

Offline-first by default

No accounts, no servers, no analytics SDKs. Your data stays on your phone. The only network call is the AdMob banner.

One job, done well

Each app does one thing — split a bill, plan a loan, calculate fuel. No bloat, no bundle of half-finished features.
