About

I build small, useful Android apps.

Indie developer behind a growing fleet of small, useful Android apps — calculators, planners and on-the-go utilities. Every app is built in Kotlin with Material 3, ships 100% offline where it can, and respects your attention with a clean ad layout.

The studio

Mobile Apps Lab is the umbrella for everything I publish on the Play Store. The mission is simple: ship one focused tool at a time, keep it offline-first, and never sneak in a subscription.

How each app gets made

  • Trend research — what real people are searching for in 2026
  • Native Kotlin build (Material 3, Android 8+ compatible)
  • AdMob banner + interstitial — never on the result screen
  • Signed release keystore, Play Console submission, Data Safety filed
  • YouTube Short demo on @MobileAppsLab

Why offline-first?

Most calculators and trackers don't need a server. Asking for an account is friction; asking for the network is worse. Every app on this site does the math on-device. Your data stays on your phone, even if Mobile Apps Lab disappears tomorrow.
