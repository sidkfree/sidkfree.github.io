Privacy policy

BabyLog Pro privacy

Effective date: 5 May 2026 · Last updated: 5 May 2026.

Summary in one paragraph

BabyLog Pro runs on your device. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data on our servers. The only data leaving your phone is the request Google AdMob makes to serve a banner or interstitial ad — and that is controlled by Google's own policies.

Data BabyLog Pro stores on your device

Calculations, profiles, history and settings you create stay locally on your Android device using the system's SharedPreferences and app-private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Data Google AdMob may collect

We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Approximate location (derived from IP)
  • Advertising ID (resettable in Android settings)
  • Device identifiers, model, OS version
  • App interactions (ad clicks, impressions)
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics

AdMob uses this data to serve relevant ads, prevent fraud, and measure performance. See Google's advertising policy for details and opt-out controls.

Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at sidkfree@gmail.com and we will remove it.

Permissions we may request

  • INTERNET — to load AdMob ads.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to detect connectivity for ad loading.
  • No camera, no contacts, no SMS, no location.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal data on our servers, there is nothing to export, modify or delete on our side. You can:

  • Reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads
  • Opt out of personalised ads on the same screen
  • Uninstall the app — that wipes the local data

Changes to this policy

If we materially change how the apps handle data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will move forward.
