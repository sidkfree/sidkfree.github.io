WarrantyBox Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

WarrantyBox Pro ("the App") is published by Sidk Youssef. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data stored on your device

All your purchases, warranties and receipt photos are stored locally on your device only (app private storage). The App works 100% offline, requires no account, and never uploads your data anywhere. Uninstalling the App deletes all of it.

Photos

When you attach a receipt photo, the App copies the image you select into its private storage. It never accesses your photo library beyond the picture you explicitly pick.

Advertising

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob data usage. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

Data we collect

We (the developer) collect no personal data. The only data processing is done by the AdMob SDK as described above (device identifiers for advertising).

Notifications

The App can show local notifications to remind you of warranties about to expire. These are generated on your device; no data leaves it.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.
