MedMinder Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

MedMinder Pro ("the App") is published by Sidk Youssef. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Not medical advice

MedMinder Pro is a personal reminder and logging tool only. It does not provide medical advice, does not diagnose or treat any condition, and does not replace your doctor or pharmacist. Always follow the instructions of a qualified health professional. The App does not recommend medications, doses or schedules — you enter your own.

Data stored on your device

All your medications, schedules, dose history and settings are stored locally on your device only (app private storage). The App works 100% offline, requires no account, and never uploads your health data anywhere. Uninstalling the App deletes all of it.

Advertising

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) to serve and measure ads. Your medication and health data are never used for advertising and never leave your device. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob data usage. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

Data we collect

We (the developer) collect no personal or health data. The only data processing is done by the AdMob SDK as described above (device identifiers for advertising).

Notifications

The App shows local reminder notifications at the times you set. They are generated on your device; no data leaves it.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.
