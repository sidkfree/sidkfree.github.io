DebtZero Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

DebtZero Pro ("the App") is published by Sidk Youssef. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data stored on your device

All your debts, payment history and settings are stored locally on your device only (app private storage). The App works 100% offline, requires no account, and never uploads your data anywhere. Uninstalling the App deletes all of it.

Not financial advice

The App is a personal planning calculator. Payoff dates and interest estimates are mathematical projections based on the numbers you enter; they are provided for information only and do not constitute financial advice, lending, or any financial service.

Advertising

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob data usage. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

Data we collect

We (the developer) collect no personal data. The only data processing is done by the AdMob SDK as described above (device identifiers for advertising).

Notifications

The App can show a local monthly reminder notification. It is generated on your device; no data leaves it.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.
