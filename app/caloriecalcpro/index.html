About CalorieCalc Pro

CalorieCalc Pro gives you a personalized daily calorie target and macro split based on the Mifflin-St Jeor formula — the same one nutritionists use. FEATURES: • Daily calorie target (BMR × activity factor) • Macro split: carbs / protein / fat in grams • Goal modes: lose fat / maintain / gain muscle • Multiple saved profiles (you, partner, kids) • History of past calculations • Metric and Imperial units • 100% offline, no signup Perfect for anyone starting a diet, cut, or bulk who wants a scientific number to aim at — without subscribing to yet another fitness app.
