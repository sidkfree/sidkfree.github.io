About Prism Blocks

Prism Blocks is the calm, glowing block puzzle you'll keep coming back to — drag prism blocks onto an 8×8 grid, complete rows and columns to clear them, and chain combos for a score that keeps climbing. No timer, no pressure, no internet. 💎 GAMEPLAY: • Drag-and-drop puzzle — three prism blocks at a time, place them anywhere they fit • Clear a full row or column to make it vanish in a burst of neon light • Combo chains — clear several lines at once for big bonus points • No timer — play one calm move or a three-hour streak • Game over only when none of your three blocks can fit • Undo your last move when you misjudge a placement • 8 unlockable colour themes at score milestones • Haptic feedback + soft ambient sounds you can toggle 🌌 DESIGN: • Pure black void background, each block a different neon spectrum colour • Smooth line-clear bursts, glow trails, gentle grid pulse • Material 3 dark menus, huge tap targets, 60 fps 🧩 WHY PRISM BLOCKS: • 100% offline — play on a flight, in a queue, anywhere • No signup, no account, no email • No tracking, no data leaves your phone • No paywall — every theme is earned, not bought • No timer, no ads mid-move — a genuinely calm puzzle EN / FR / AR fully translated. Built for the 30-second break and the long quiet evening alike.
