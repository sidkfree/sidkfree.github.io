About Arrow Out

Arrow Out is a quiet snake-escape puzzle for people who like the satisfying click of a clean solve. Tap a snake; it slithers off the board if the corridor ahead of its head is clear of other snakes. Tap a blocked one and you lose a heart. Plan the order, free the grid, three-star every level. 🐍 GAMEPLAY: • Tap a snake — it slithers off if the path ahead is clear • Blocked tap costs a heart; out of hearts = retry • Every level is generated by reverse placement, so every level is solvable • 60 hand-curated levels across 4 difficulty tiers — Beginner to Master • Earn 1, 2 or 3 stars per level — flawless solves are 3 stars • Undo your last tap when you misjudge a chain • Local progress, no leaderboards, no rush 🎨 DESIGN: • Deep navy void background, neon snake bodies with directional glow • Smooth slither animation, satisfying clear-burst • Material 3 dark menus, huge tap targets, 60 fps 🔒 RESPECTS YOU: • 100% offline — play on a flight, in a queue, anywhere • No signup, no account, no email • No tracking — nothing leaves your phone • No paywall — every level is earned by playing EN / FR / AR fully translated. Built for the quiet 5-minute brain-stretch.
