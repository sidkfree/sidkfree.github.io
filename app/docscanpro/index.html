About DocScan Pro

DocScan Pro turns your phone into a powerful document scanner — multi-page PDFs, on-device OCR text extraction, smart edge detection, and signatures — without an account, without a subscription, and without your documents leaving your phone. FEATURES: • Multi-page document scanning with auto edge detection • On-device OCR (text extraction) — works fully offline • Smart filters: black and white, color, grayscale, magic enhance • Crop, rotate and reorder pages with one tap • Export as PDF or JPEG — share, email, print • Add a hand-drawn signature to any page • Searchable PDF library — find any document by extracted text • Folders to organize receipts, IDs, contracts, notes • Compress PDFs for tiny email-friendly file sizes • Password-protect any document • Share PDF file or share extracted text as plain text • 100% offline — your documents never leave your phone • Material 3 design with pure-black dark theme Perfect for freelancers, students, travellers and anyone tired of CamScanner watermarks and forced subscriptions.
