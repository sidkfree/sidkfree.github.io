About SpendLog Pro

SpendLog Pro is a fast, beautiful expense tracker for people who want to actually see where their money goes — no bank login, no cloud, no nonsense. 💸 LOG IN SECONDS: • Tap +, pick a category, enter the amount — done in under 3 seconds • 16 built-in categories, fully customizable • Optional note + date picker for past entries 📊 BUDGETS THAT WORK: • Global monthly budget + per-category caps • Live gauge on the home screen — green / amber / red • Smart alerts at 80% / 100% of any cap 🔁 RECURRING & SUBSCRIPTIONS: • Track Netflix, rent, gym, internet, insurance — anything that hits monthly/weekly/yearly • Auto-injected into your monthly view on the right date • One screen to see ALL your subscriptions + total monthly cost 📈 INSIGHTS: • Pie chart by category for the current month • Bar chart of last 6 / 12 months • Top 5 categories + daily average + projected month-end 🌍 MULTI-CURRENCY: • MAD, EUR, USD, GBP, AED, SAR — 30+ currencies with proper locale formatting 🎨 NEON-DARK DESIGN: • Deep black background with mint→cyan gradient accents • Glowing pill buttons, soft card surfaces, Material 3 dark 🔒 100% YOURS: • Fully offline — no account, no sign-up, no cloud • No bank linking, no permissions you don't need • Export everything as CSV anytime; local backup + restore EN / FR / AR fully translated.
