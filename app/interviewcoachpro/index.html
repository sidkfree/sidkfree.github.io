About InterviewCoach Pro

InterviewCoach Pro is a calm, private way to prepare for a job interview — 200+ real questions, STAR-method answer builder, timed mock sessions, offline answer library. FEATURES: • 200+ behavioural, technical and HR questions across 10 role tracks • STAR-method answer builder — Situation, Task, Action, Result with character counts • Save and re-edit per question — personal interview playbook • Timed mock sessions — 90s answer window, score your delivery • Bookmark hard questions, filter by role/level/category • Recent mocks history with average score • Pulsing mic + concentric rings animation while you speak • 100% offline — your career notes stay on your phone • Material 3 dark theme with neon-blue accents • EN / FR / AR fully translated
