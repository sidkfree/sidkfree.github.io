About Neon Glider

Neon Glider is a one-tap arcade runner built for that 'just one more run' feeling — pilot a glowing glider through endless neon energy gates, ride the combo multiplier, and climb your own high-score wall. Pure synthwave, instant restart, zero internet. ⚡ GAMEPLAY: • One-tap controls — tap to pulse up, release to glide down. Master it in seconds, chase it for hours. • Endless neon gates — procedurally generated, speed ramps the longer you survive • Combo multiplier — thread the gates tight for near-miss bonuses • Daily Challenge — one seeded run shared by everyone that day • Glider skins — unlock 12 gliders at score milestones • Local leaderboard + best-run chart • Haptic feedback + synthwave soundtrack you can toggle 🌌 DESIGN: • Full synthwave aesthetic — pure black void, neon cyan and magenta glow, scrolling grid horizon • Particle trail behind your glider, chromatic-aberration flash on near-misses • 60 fps smooth glide on any modern phone 🎮 WHY NEON GLIDER: • 100% offline — play on a flight, in the metro, anywhere • No signup, no account, no email • No tracking, no data leaves your phone • No paywall — every glider is earned, not bought EN / FR / AR fully translated. Built for the 30-second queue and the 3-hour streak alike.
