About LoanCalc Pro

LoanCalc Pro is a no-nonsense loan and mortgage calculator built for anyone shopping for a car, house or personal loan. FEATURES: • Monthly payment calculation (P&I) • Full amortization schedule — month by month • Compare two loans side by side • Total interest paid + payoff date • Extra payment simulator • 100% offline, no account required Whether you're a first-time home buyer or just running numbers on a car loan, LoanCalc Pro gives you a clear answer in seconds.
