About LunaMatch

LunaMatch is a calm, futuristic 3D memory game played in moonlight. Flip pairs of glowing cards on a 3D-lit board with real shadows and ambient moon glow — sleek, dark, and satisfying. 🌙 GAMEPLAY: • Classic memory match — tap two cards, find the pair, clear the board • Real-time 3D card flips with directional moonlight + cast shadows • Multi-level: 3×4 starter grids all the way to 6×6 expert boards • Earn up to 3 stars per level based on moves used • Unlockable themes — symbol packs (geometric, cosmic, nature, runes) • Smooth haptics + subtle ambient soundtrack — both toggleable 📶 BLUETOOTH HEAD-TO-HEAD: • Play 1-vs-1 with a friend, phone-to-phone over Bluetooth — no internet, no account, no Wi-Fi • One player hosts, the other joins; take turns flipping pairs • Find a pair → score and go again; miss → next player's turn • Most pairs at the end wins 🎨 DESIGN: • Dark futuristic aesthetic — deep navy bg, silver moonlight, glowing card edges, sharp shadows • 60 fps Canvas-rendered 3D with directional lighting + per-card shadow projection • Material 3 menus, big tap targets 🔒 RESPECTS YOU: • 100% offline — no internet ever needed (Bluetooth is local-only) • No account, no sign-up, no email • No tracking — nothing leaves your phone • No paywall — every level and theme is earned by playing EN / FR / AR fully translated.
