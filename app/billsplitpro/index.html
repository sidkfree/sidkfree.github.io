About BillSplit Pro

BillSplit Pro is the fastest way to split a restaurant bill, hotel room or group outing between friends. Type the total, set the tip, split evenly or by share — done. FEATURES: • Split evenly or by custom share per person • Tip calculator with country defaults (US 18%, EU 10%, JP 0%) • Multi-currency converter built in • Share the result via WhatsApp / Messages in one tap • 100% offline — no signup, no tracking • Clean Material 3 dark UI Perfect for couples, roomates, group trips and casual meals.
