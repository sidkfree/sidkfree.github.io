About NapPro Watch

NapPro Watch uses your Galaxy Watch's heart-rate sensor and accelerometer to detect when you've actually fallen asleep, then wakes you at the end of a light-sleep window — usually 10-20 minutes after dozing off — so you wake refreshed, never groggy. FEATURES: • Smart wake during light sleep cycle • Adjustable nap window: 10/20/30 min max • Gentle haptic + sunrise watch face wake • Recovery score (1-100) after each nap • Nap streak + weekly history • Standalone — no phone needed during the nap • 100% offline — sleep data stays on your wrist • Battery friendly (~3% per session) Perfect for shift workers, jet-lagged travelers, tired parents, and anyone who wants a real rest in 20 minutes.
