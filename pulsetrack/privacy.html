PulseTrack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2026

PulseTrack ("the app") is a blood-pressure tracking and education tool. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data you enter stays on your device

Your blood-pressure readings, pulse, tags, medication notes and settings are stored only on your device. PulseTrack has no account, no login, and does not upload, sync, or transmit your health data to us or any server. We (the developer) cannot see your data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

PulseTrack displays ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect and process certain information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and general device information, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). We do not share the health data you enter with advertisers.

Permissions

Internet/network access is used only to load ads. Notifications (optional) are used for measurement reminders you enable yourself.

Medical disclaimer

PulseTrack is a tracking and education tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or recommend changing any treatment. Always consult your doctor.

Children

PulseTrack is not directed at children under 13.
