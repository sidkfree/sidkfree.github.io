ShiftPay — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 May 2026

ShiftPay ("the app") is a work-hours and pay tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Your data stays on your device

Your jobs, shifts, pay rates, earnings, notes and settings are stored only on your device. ShiftPay has no account, no login, and does not upload, sync, or transmit your data to us or any server. Timesheets and backups (PDF/CSV/JSON) are generated locally and shared only when you choose to share them. Uninstalling the app deletes its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

ShiftPay displays ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect and process certain information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and general device information, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). We do not share the work/pay data you enter with advertisers.

Permissions

Internet/network access is used only to load ads.

Children

ShiftPay is not directed at children under 13.
